## Child Safety Standards & Anti-EASI Policy ##

**Effective Date:** July 22, 2026  
**Application:** Tieddy  
**Developer:** Christian Eduardo

---

## Overview and Commitment
The Service Provider is committed to providing a safe environment for all users. We maintain a **zero-tolerance policy** regarding Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (EASI). 

The Application strictly prohibits the generation, uploading, sharing, or distribution of any content, images, drawings, or text that depicts, promotes, or facilitates child exploitation or abuse.

---

## Content Moderation & Action
If any user-generated content or account activity is found to violate this policy:
* **Immediate Termination:** The responsible user account will be permanently suspended and banned from the service without prior notice.
* **Content Removal:** All associated user-generated content (including shared drawings, notes, and photos) will be immediately purged from our active systems.
* **Law Enforcement Escalation:** Any incident involving CSAM/EASI will be reported to appropriate law enforcement authorities and the **National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)** or relevant international bodies, along with necessary identifying technical data.

---

## Reporting & Designated Contact
We encourage users to report any concerns, suspicious activity, or potential violations immediately.

* **In-App Reporting:** Users can report inappropriate content directly within the Tieddy app via the content moderation options.
* **Direct Email Contact:** You may contact our designated Child Safety representative directly at:  
  **`support.tieddy@gmail.com`**

Reports regarding child safety are treated with the highest priority and will be reviewed promptly by the Service Provider.
